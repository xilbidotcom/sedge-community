# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive in issues, reviews and discussions.
Critique technical work rather than people. Harassment, discriminatory language,
threats, disclosure of private information and unsolicited promotion are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content or restrict
participation in project spaces. Decisions should be proportionate and based
on behaviour. Report concerns privately through the contact channel on the
[XILBI website](https://www.xilbi.com/); do not disclose another person's private
information in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly.

This policy covers project-managed spaces and people explicitly representing
the project. It does not promise a staffed service or a fixed response time.
